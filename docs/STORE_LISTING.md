# Subscription Tracker: full description

**Subscription Tracker** helps you keep all of your recurring expenses in one simple, calm, and completely clutter-free place.

Modern life renews itself quietly in the background. Netflix renews while you sleep. Amazon Prime, Spotify, YouTube Premium, iCloud, your Wi-Fi and broadband, your mobile plan, the gym, the apps you tried once and forgot. Each one takes a small amount, on its own schedule, whether you are thinking about it or not. Individually they feel like nothing. Together, they are a second rent that nobody ever sat down and agreed to. Subscription Tracker exists to give you that number, clearly, quietly, and always up to date, so every renewal becomes a choice you made, not a surprise you found.

**Add everything you pay for, in seconds.**
Add subscriptions and regular bills alike, from streaming services, music, internet, mobile plans, cloud storage, software, fitness, news and gaming to memberships of any kind, and enter what each one costs, how often it bills (weekly, monthly, every three months, or yearly), and the date it next renews. Gentle starting points for familiar names help you begin, and every detail can be changed at any time. There is no bank connection to grant, no statement to upload, and no account to create.

**See where your money is actually going.**
The moment you add a subscription, the app keeps a quiet, running picture of your recurring life: what you spend each month once everything is counted the same way, what that becomes over a full year, which renewal is coming next and exactly how many days away it is, and how the next six months look month by month. A simple, optional monthly budget fills the main card gently as you approach it. It is information, never an alarm. Due-soon renewals rise to the top on their own; everything else waits below in calm date order.

**One gentle reminder, while there is still time.**
Before any payment is due, Subscription Tracker sends a single, quiet reminder on the day, or one, three, or seven days before, at nine in the morning. You choose per subscription. That is the moment to ask the only question that matters: do I still want this? Keep it, and do nothing. Pause it, if life has changed for a while. Cancel it, while cancelling is still free. No feeds, no streaks, no red badges shouting for attention. Just one nudge, early enough to act on.

**Designed to be looked at for a moment, then put down.**
The interface takes its cue from quiet, editorial design: a deep, restful black, one soft cream card, fine hairlines, small unhurried labels, and large, light numbers that are easy to read at a glance. Nothing competes for your attention, because the app wants a few seconds of it, not minutes. Marking a payment as paid rolls it forward to its next cycle in one tap. Pausing silences a subscription without deleting it. Search and simple filters find anything instantly when your list grows.

**Private by construction, not by promise.**
Everything you enter lives only on your phone, saved in the app's own on-device storage. There is no sign-in, no cloud copy, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind. Your data never leaves the device.

Subscription Tracker is for anyone who has ever opened a bank statement and wondered where it all went. Add what you pay for. See it clearly, once. And let a calm reminder, arriving quietly before each renewal, turn forgotten subscriptions back into money that is yours to direct.

*Calm. Subtle. Clutter-free.*
