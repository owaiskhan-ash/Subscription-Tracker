About

Subscription Tracker helps you keep all your recurring expenses in one simple, unhurried place. Add the subscriptions and regular bills that quietly renew in the background of modern life — Netflix, Amazon Prime, Spotify, Wi-Fi and broadband, mobile plans, cloud storage, apps, gyms and memberships — along with what each one costs, how often it bills, and when it is next due.

The app keeps a calm, running picture of where your money is going: what you spend each month, what that becomes over a year, which renewals are approaching, and how the next six months look. Before any payment is due, you receive one gentle reminder — early enough to keep, pause, cancel, or adjust anything you no longer need, while there is still time. No feeds, no streaks, no noise. Just a clear count, kept quietly for you.
